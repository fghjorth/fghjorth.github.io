Research snapshot: 11 May 2026

Weekly gymnastics options for a 6-year-old and a 9-year-old near Sankt Hans Torv

Ranked by proximity, weekday fit, likely age fit, and whether both children can train at roughly the same time. The key caveat is that most clubs have not yet published their full fall 2026 / 2026-27 programs.

Bottom Line

The closest practical targets are GF81, HKG, and ODK. For truly simultaneous age-separated spring classes, Gymnastikforeningen Hermes has the cleanest current pattern, but it is much farther away in Flintholm. For fall 2026, set reminders for mid-June, 1 July, and early August 2026 because several clubs only publish or open registration shortly before the season.

1. GF81, Institut for Idræt

Nørre Allé 57, Copenhagen N; about 0.8-1.0 km from Sankt Hans Torv

GF81 is very close and inexpensive. The current schedule lists Spring Mini for ages 4-9 and Spring Max for ages 9-14, with weekday slots. This is the most convenient option if the fall 2026 schedule resembles the current one.

Current relevant classes
Spring Mini 4-9: Mon 16:30-18:00; Spring Max 9-14: Tue 16:30-18:00; TeamGym 9+: Wed 17:30-19:00. Thursday expansion is listed if demand is high.
Both at same time?
Possible if Thursday age-group expansion runs; otherwise close but not same day.
Cost
Season price listed as 700 kr.
Registration
No fall 2026 opening/closing date published. Current site says trial lessons via Motivu, then enrollment in Conventus; likely first-come/full.

Action: email GF81 in June and ask whether Spring Mini and Spring Max will run in parallel on Thursdays in fall 2026.

Sources: GF81 front page, GF81 hold schedule, GF81 location and holidays.

Closest Weekday Fall 2026 not published

2. HKG Gymnastik, Blågård Skole

Hans Tavsens Gade 4, Copenhagen N; about 0.5-0.7 km from Sankt Hans Torv

HKG is the nearest club and has beginner spring from age 6. It is less clearly age-separated than ODK or Hermes, but it is very practical geographically.

Current relevant classes
Spring piger begynder from age 6: Wed 17:45-19:15. Spring øvede from age 7: Tue 17:45-19:15. Børnehold 4-6: Mon 16:30-17:45.
Both at same time?
Yes, if both attend the same beginner spring class. Not ideal if you want separate age groups.
Cost
Spring classes listed at 700 kr.; 4-6 children’s half-year class listed at 400 kr.
Registration
Fall 2026 dates not published. Current info says active gymnasts get email, new gymnasts create a profile, and HKG holds open house on the last Sunday in August.

Action: treat HKG as the easiest fallback. Check in late August; if they repeat the open-house pattern, that would be Sunday 30 August 2026.

Sources: HKG front page, HKG program, HKG parent information.

Strong weekday choice Good registration info Often waitlisted

3. Gymnastikforeningen ODK

Skolen ved Søerne, Hermeshallen, Skolen på La Cours Vej; roughly 0.9-2.3 km depending on venue

ODK has many age-specific children’s gymnastics and spring classes, including several weekday options. The trade-off is that many relevant classes are consecutive rather than simultaneous, and popular classes may already be waitlisted.

Current relevant classes
Springpiger 0.-1. kl. Tue 16:30-17:30 or 17:30-18:30; Springpiger 2.-3. kl. Tue 17:30-19:00 or 18:30-19:30; Spring/rytme 0.-2. kl. Thu 17:30-19:00; ODK Kids 7-9 Sun 12:00-13:15.
Both at same time?
Usually no. Best fit is consecutive Tuesday classes at the same venue, or one mixed 6-8/7-9 class depending on school year.
Cost
Relevant current prices range from 825 kr. to 1,850 kr.; short summer-gymnastics listings are 250 kr.
Registration
For 2025/26, the list started updating from 1 July 2025. Børnegymnastik opened 6 Aug 2025 at 18:00 for returning members and 13 Aug 2025 at 18:00 for new members. Fall 2026 dates not yet published.

Action: this is the best organized registration target. Check from 1 July 2026, then be ready for the likely early-August opening window.

Sources: ODK gymnastics overview, ODK enrollment page.

Best simultaneous age split Farther away

4. Gymnastikforeningen Hermes

Flintholm Svømmehal / KU.BE, Frederiksberg; about 4.5-5.5 km by bike from Sankt Hans Torv

Hermes has the clearest current setup for two children in different age groups at exactly the same time: Spring 6-8 and Spring 8-10 currently run in parallel on weekdays. It is much less convenient geographically.

Current relevant classes
Spring 6-8 and Spring 8-10: Mon 16:00-17:00 and Wed 16:00-17:00. KIG 6-8 and KIG 8-10: Mon 17:00-18:30.
Both at same time?
Yes, based on current schedule. This is the cleanest age-separated option.
Cost
Spring 6-8 / 8-10 listed at 2,800 kr.; KIG 6-8 / 8-10 listed at 3,500 kr.
Registration
Fall 2026 dates not published. Current program says new-member registrations before 16 June 2025 at 18:00 could be annulled; no closing date listed beyond full/waitlist.

Action: use Hermes if simultaneous classes matter more than distance. Check around mid-June 2026 for new-member timing.

Sources: Hermes program, Hermes hold overview, Hermes Motivu profile.
